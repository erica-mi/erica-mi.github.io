Erica Mi

Hi! My name is Erica - welcome to my page ☺︎

I am a first-year NSF CISE CSGrad4US Fellow, Ph.D. student, and M.S. student in Computer Science, Human-Computer Interaction at the University of Chicago, advised by Mina Lee. I received my M.S. in Informatics from the College of Information Sciences and Technology at Penn State University, advised by Fred Fonseca, my B.S. in Information Sciences and Technology, and B.A. in Telecommunications through the Schreyer Honors College at Penn State.

My research interest is in human-AI interaction, particularly understanding how interaction behaviors reveal individuals’ AI competence and ability to use AI effectively.
